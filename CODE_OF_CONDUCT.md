# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating
empathy and kindness, respecting differing opinions and experiences, giving and gracefully
accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory
comments, publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct which
could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All
community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any
incident.
